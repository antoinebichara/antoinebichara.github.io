How I score

Every visit gets three scores out of 10, which add up to one overall Value score.

FoodHow good it tastes.
PriceHow fair the price is for what you get, whether it's a $3 taco or a tasting menu. Higher is better.
AtmosphereThe room, the service, the feeling of being there.

Value = 40% Food + 40% Price + 20% Atmosphere
