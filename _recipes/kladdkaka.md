## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 2 1/2 tsp butter, melted
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup flour
- 2 tsp vanilla
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 large eggs

**Total Time: 70 minutes**

## Steps

1. Grease an 8inch cake dish. Preheat oven to 300 degrees Fahrenheit. Mix together flour, cocoa powder and salt. In another bowl beat together sugar, eggs and vanilla they form a cohesive mixture. Slowly add the flour mix to the eggs, stirring continuously. Then pour in melted butter, continuing to stir. Pour contents into baking dish and bake for 20 minutes. Then let sit for another 20 minutes before serving with some fresh strawberries or raspberries.
