## Ingredients

- 6-8 endives, with any brown leaves removed
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1.5 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 2 tbsp butter

**Serves Two · Total Time: 45 minutes**

## Steps

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/endives-2.jpg" alt="">

1. In a dutch oven or large pot, melt the butter over low heat. Once starting to brown slightly, add in the endives in one layer in the pot.
1. Sprinkle with the salt, sugar and lemon juice. Then pour the white wine, and a dash of water in between the endives, so as not to wash off the salt and sugar.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/endives-3.jpg" alt="">

{: start="3"}
1. Cover the pot, reduce heat to low, and simmer for at least 40 minutes, more or less depending on the size of the endives.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/endives-4.jpg" alt="">

{: start="4"}
1. Then remove the lid, increase heat to medium and cook until all the liquid is gone and the bottom sides are browned. Then flip over the endives and brown the other side. Serve hot.
