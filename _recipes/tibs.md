## Ingredients

- 2 large white onions, chopped
- 2 inch ginger root, minced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 3 tbsp berbere spice
- 1.5 lbs beef sirloin, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp canola oil

<!-- next group -->

- Serve with fresh injera

**Serves Four · Total Time: 45 minutes**

## Steps

1. In a large pan over medium-low heat, melt the butter, then add the onions, berbere, garlic and ginger.
1. Cook for at least 30 minutes, only stirring every few minutes, until the onions are golden in color.
1. Then blend the mixture to a puree and return to the pan. Season and keep warm.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/tibs-2.jpeg" alt="">

{: start="4"}
1. Generously season the beef with salt and pepper. Add canola oil to a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat and wait until smoking. Then add the beef in batches, searing for about two minutes on each side. After all the beef is cooked, add to the beef to the sauce, squeeze in lemon juice and serve on warm injera.
