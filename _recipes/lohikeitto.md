## Ingredients

- 2 leeks, sliced
- 1.5 lbs potatoes, cut into large cubes
- 2 carrots, roughly sliced
- 1.5 lbs salmon, cut into large pieces
- 4 cups fish or seafood stock
- 1 cup half and half
- 1 bunch dill
- 4 tbsp butter

**Serves Four · Total Time: 45 minutes**

## Steps

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/lohikeitto-2.jpg" alt="">

1. In a large pot, melt the butter over medium-low heat and cook the leeks for about 6 minutes or until soft and slightly browned.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/lohikeitto-3.jpg" alt="">

{: start="2"}
1. Add in the potatoes, carrots and fish stock. Bring to a boil and then simmer for about 12 minutes or until the vegetables are almost done.
1. Add in the salmon and cream and simmer for another 5 minutes or until the salmon is fully cooked.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/lohikeitto-4.jpg" alt="">

{: start="4"}
1. Season generously with salt and pepper.
1. Serve hot with the fresh plucked dill.
