## Ingredients

- 2 lbs chicken breast
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 3 tbsp. graham masala
- 4 tbsp. chili powder
- 1 tbsp. cumin
- 0.5 tbsp. turmeric
- 1 tbsp. cayenne
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 cups tomato sauce
- 1.5 cups water
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 small fresh ginger root, grated
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp honey
- Handful of fresh cilantro
- Handful of sliced almonds (optional)

**Serves Four · Total Time: 45 minutes**

## Steps

1. Cut the chicken into small pieces and season with salt and pepper. In a bowl, add the turmeric and 2 tbsp chili powder to the chicken and mix thorughly.
1. Add 1 tbsp butter to a pot at high heat and add in half the chicken, searing all sides for about 5 minutes, then move to a plate. Repeat with the second half of chicken and move to the same plate.
1. In the same pot add another 1 tbsp butter, the onion, almonds, graham masala, 2 tbsp chili powder, cayenne, cumin, ginger, cinammon, garlic and 1 tbsp salt and pepper. Mix thoroughly for a few minutes.
1. Add in the tomato sauce, water, heavy cream, 3 tbsp butter and the chicken. Cover the pot and simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionaly. Serve with rice and top off with fresh cilantro.
