## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs extra lean ground beef
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, half chopped, half plucked (for serving)
- 1 tsp ground allspice
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cummin
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges (for serving)
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 4 large flour tortillas, or same-shaped fresh pizza dough, thinly rolled

**Serves Four · Total Time: 35 minutes**

## Steps

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/lahmacun-2.jpg" alt="">

1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees Fahrenheit.
1. In a large bowl combine the meat and spices. Mix thoroughly.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/lahmacun-3.jpg" alt="">

{: start="3"}
1. Cover a cooking tray with parchment paper, spread out the four bases, and spread the mixture evenly onto each in one thin layer.

<img class="img-responsive" src="img/lahmacun-4.jpg" alt="">

{: start="4"}
1. Bake for about 6 minutes or until the outside of the dough begins to brown. Serve hot with the lemon and plucked parsley.
