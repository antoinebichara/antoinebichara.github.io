## Ingredients

### Vegetable Stock

- 3 yellow onions, sliced
- 1 yellow onion, halved and charred
- 4 carrots, peeled, sliced
- 3 celery sticks, halved
- 1/2 bunch fresh thyme
- 3 leeks, just white part, sliced
- 4 quarts water

### Salmon and Broth

- 2 lbs salmon fillet
- 5 large Roma tomatoes, chopped
- 1 yellow onion, sliced
- 2 serrano chilis, minced
- 3 cups vegetable stock
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 tsp saffron threads
- Handful of fresh cilantro

**Vegetable Stock · Total Time: 3 hours**

## Steps

1. Put all the ingredients in a pot, bring to a boil, and then simmer for 2 hours and 45 minutes. Pour through fine sieve. Excess stock can be frozen.

### Salmon and Broth · Serves Four People · Total Time: 45 Minutes

1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
1. Add 2 tbsp olive oil, onion and chilis into a pot on low heat and cook for 8 minutes. Add the tomatoes, vegetable stock, white wine and saffron threads, bring to a boil, then simmer for 25 minutes.
1. While broth is simmering, season salmon with salt, pepper and coriander.
1. Add 1 tbsp butter to a pan on high heat and then place the salmon skin side down. Cook for three minutes, while drizzling excess butter in pan back over the salmon with a spoon.
1. Then transfer pan with salmon to the oven for another 8 minutes.
1. Meanwhile, pour the contents of the pot with the broth into a blender, and blend very briefly before passing blended contents through a fine sieve.
1. In a large flat bowl, pour out some of the broth, place salmon in the center and finish with the cilantro.
