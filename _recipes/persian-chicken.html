Persian Saffron Chicken Ingredients:

1 whole chicken cut into pieces
1 large sweet onion
1 tsp saffron
1 tbsp tumeric
1 lemon, squeezed
2 tbsp grated ginger
1 cinammon stick
1/3 cup butter
1 tsp sugar
