--- title: "Israeli Salad" date: 2017-04-08 image: salad.jpg ---

1 large cucumber, chopped
3 fresh tomatoes, chopped
1 bunch parsley, chopped
2 tbsp lemon juice or 1 tbsp white vinegar
3 tbsp olive oil
2 tsp salt

Makes four small portions       Total Time: 10 minutes
Mix ingredients together. Done. This is a great side dish to have available.
